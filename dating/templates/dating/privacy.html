Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile Data: Name, age, gender, photos, and bio.
  • Usage Data: Swipes, matches, and messages.
  • Device Data: IP address and device type for security.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to recommend potential matches and improve the matching algorithm. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Payment Information

LOVENY does not store your debit/credit card information. All transactions are handled directly by our payment partners (Paystack & Flutterwave), who are PCI-DSS compliant.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and data at any time via the Settings menu or by contacting support.
